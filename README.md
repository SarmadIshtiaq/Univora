# Univora

> **AI-Powered University Discovery & Graduate Application Intelligence**

Univora is a Multi-Agentic AI and automation system designed to reduce the repetitive research involved in exploring graduate education opportunities.

The system is being built from a real problem: manually researching universities, programs, faculty, professor research, requirements, application information, and follow-ups across many different websites.

## Initial Goal

Starting from a structured list of USA universities, Univora will automatically:

```text
University
    ↓
Programs
    ↓
Faculty
    ↓
Professor Research
    ↓
Requirements
    ↓
Evidence
```

The objective is to replace repetitive manual research with an automated, evidence-based workflow.

## Initial Scope

The first version focuses on:

* University discovery
* Program discovery
* Faculty discovery
* Professor research
* Requirements extraction
* Evidence collection

Future versions may add:

* Application assistance
* Research direction support
* CV/proposal/email assistance
* Outreach tracking
* Follow-up automation
* Research matching
* Recommendation
* Feedback-driven intelligence

## Core Principle

> **Human chooses. AI researches. Automation handles repetitive work.**
